FORGE model

FORGE compares the total cost of building a custom AI accelerator with buying workload-equivalent vendor GPUs. Defaults are illustrative, not quotes or forecasts.

Model parameter definitions

The model begins with six groups of inputs:

  • Fleet and performance: first-year vendor-equivalent fleet, annual demand growth, TCO horizon, useful custom-to-vendor performance, vendor GPU price including HBM, and its annual price change.
  • HBM: custom HBM price and annual change; custom/vendor stack counts, capacity, and bandwidth; custom procurement overhead; and power per stack.
  • Power: vendor and custom logic/module power excluding HBM, plus host/network power per device.
  • Logic manufacturing: foundry and node descriptors, wafer diameter and price, annual wafer-price change, die area, known-good yield, and mask/process NRE.
  • Packaging and engineering: package/interposer cost, final package yield, board/test cost, design NRE, and initial and ongoing software costs.
  • Datacenter economics: electricity, PUE, platform CAPEX, facility allocation, annual power/cooling capacity, and vendor support.

Every symbol, default, and precise definition is listed in the calculator glossary and the canonical model document.

Model details

The decision metric is A = TCO_BUY − TCO_BUILD: BUILD wins when A > 0. Demand compounds annually, and the custom fleet divides vendor-equivalent demand by useful performance. Only incremental fleet growth purchases hardware.

BUILD estimates gross and known-good dies per wafer, then combines yield-adjusted logic, HBM, and package/interposer costs with board and test. Fixed BUILD NRE is design, initial software, and masks/tooling.

Both paths include device purchases, platform and facility CAPEX, continuous electricity at the selected PUE, annual power/cooling capacity, and software. Vendor HBM is embedded in the vendor GPU price; BUILD HBM is itemized. The model also reports break-even fleet, ledgers, pie charts, and a +20% one-at-a-time sensitivity.

Illustrative default result

The four-year baseline produces $4.8197B BUY TCO and $2.4161B BUILD TCO, a $2.4036B BUILD advantage. This result depends strongly on the 50,000-device starting scale, $35,000 vendor price, $800M fixed BUILD NRE, and 1.5× useful-performance assumption.
